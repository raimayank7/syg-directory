South-Western City: public schools

34 schools

Ohio, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 34 public schools in the South-Western City district in Ohio: 16 elementary, 10 middle, 5 high, 3 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 21,813 students in 34 of them; the median school has 552 students and the largest has 1,864. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 6th largest of the 1,003 districts in Ohio by number of public schools, and holds 0.95% of the state's 3,577 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 4 towns, most in Grove City (18), Columbus (11), Galloway (4), Grove (1).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 9 of the 34 schools offer prekindergarten and 16 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 5 to 16 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 5 to 5, and for grades 9 to 12 from 5 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 34 of the 34 schools of this district. The middle school has 552 students; a quarter of them have 440 or fewer and a quarter have 698 or more. The smallest count is 0 (South-Western Career Academy) and the largest is 1,864 (Westland High School). 2 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 5 ZIP codes, the most schools in 43123 (19). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
