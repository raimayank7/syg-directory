Kent School District: public schools

46 schools

Washington, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 46 public schools in the Kent School District district in Washington: 29 elementary, 8 high, 7 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 25,700 students in 46 of them; the median school has 420 students and the largest has 2,124. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 5th largest of the 331 districts in Washington by number of public schools, and holds 1.80% of the state's 2,553 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Kent (32), Covington (7), Renton (5), Black Diamond (1).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 27 of the 46 schools offer prekindergarten and 29 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 29 to 30 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 9 to 9, and for grades 9 to 12 from 8 to 9. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 46 of the 46 schools of this district. The middle school has 420 students; a quarter of them have 332 or fewer and a quarter have 656 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Birth to Age 2) and the largest is 2,124 (Kentridge High School). 3 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 7 ZIP codes, the most schools in 98042 (13). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
