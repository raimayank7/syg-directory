Sumter: public schools

13 schools

Florida, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Sumter district in Florida: 5 elementary, 5 other, 2 high, 1 middle. 1 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 9,597 students in 13 of them; the median school has 658 students and the largest has 3,728. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 46th largest of the 76 districts in Florida by number of public schools, and holds 0.31% of the state's 4,229 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 5 towns, most in Bushnell (6), Wildwood (3), Webster (2), Lake Panasoffkee (1).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 6 of the 13 schools offer prekindergarten and 8 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 9 to 10 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 7 to 9, and for grades 9 to 12 from 7 to 7. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 13 of the 13 schools of this district. The middle school has 658 students; a quarter of them have 90 or fewer and a quarter have 738 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Contracted Residential Services) and the largest is 3,728 (Villages Charter School). 4 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Sumter District Office (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 5 ZIP codes, the most schools in 33513 (6). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
