Onslow County Schools: public schools

39 schools

North Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 39 public schools in the Onslow County Schools district in North Carolina: 22 elementary, 8 high, 8 middle, 1 other. The file reports 27,592 students in 39 of them; the median school has 632 students and the largest has 1,449. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 336 districts in North Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 1.44% of the state's 2,716 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 7 towns, most in Jacksonville (25), Richlands (4), Swansboro (4), Holly Ridge (3).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 6 of the 39 schools offer prekindergarten and 23 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 23 to 23 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 9 to 9, and for grades 9 to 12 from 9 to 9. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 39 of the 39 schools of this district. The middle school has 632 students; a quarter of them have 520 or fewer and a quarter have 888 or more. The smallest count is 187 (Onslow Early College) and the largest is 1,449 (Jacksonville High). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 8 ZIP codes, the most schools in 28540 (15). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
