Lake and Peninsula Borough School District: public schools

11 schools

Alaska, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 11 public schools in the Lake and Peninsula Borough School District district in Alaska: 11 other. The file reports 332 students in 11 of them; the median school has 22 students and the largest has 102. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 11th largest of the 54 districts in Alaska by number of public schools, and holds 2.23% of the state's 494 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 11 towns, most in Chignik Lagoon (1), Chignik Lake (1), Igiugig (1), Kokhanok (1).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 11 of the 11 schools offer prekindergarten and 11 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 11 to 11 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 11 to 11, and for grades 9 to 12 from 11 to 11. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 11 of the 11 schools of this district. The middle school has 22 students; a quarter of them have 19 or fewer and a quarter have 35 or more. The smallest count is 10 (Levelock School) and the largest is 102 (Newhalen School). 10 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 9 ZIP codes, the most schools in 99613 (2). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
