St. Louis City: public schools

68 schools

Missouri, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 68 public schools in the St. Louis City district in Missouri: 43 elementary, 15 high, 8 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 career and technical schools. The file reports 18,284 students in 68 of them; the median school has 265 students and the largest has 816. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 1st largest of the 565 districts in Missouri by number of public schools, and holds 2.75% of the state's 2,475 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in St Louis (60), St. Louis (7), St.Louis (1).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 40 of the 68 schools offer prekindergarten and 44 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 43 to 44 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 18 to 32, and for grades 9 to 12 from 15 to 17. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 68 of the 68 schools of this district. The middle school has 265 students; a quarter of them have 165 or fewer and a quarter have 369 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Educ Therap Support At Madison) and the largest is 816 (Gateway High). 9 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 16 ZIP codes, the most schools in 63106 (10). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
