West St. Paul-Mendota Heights-Eagan: public schools

13 schools

Minnesota, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the West St. Paul-Mendota Heights-Eagan district in Minnesota: 5 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 5,293 students in 13 of them; the median school has 378 students and the largest has 1,648. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 41st largest of the 550 districts in Minnesota by number of public schools, and holds 0.48% of the state's 2,696 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 3 towns, most in Mendota Heights (7), West Saint Paul (5), Eagan (1).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 4 of the 13 schools offer prekindergarten and 6 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 3 to 6 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 4 to 4, and for grades 9 to 12 from 4 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 13 of the 13 schools of this district. The middle school has 378 students; a quarter of them have 21 or fewer and a quarter have 402 or more. The smallest count is 0 (W St. Paul Area Learning Cntr.) and the largest is 1,648 (Two Rivers High School). 4 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 3 ZIP codes, the most schools in 55118 (11). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
