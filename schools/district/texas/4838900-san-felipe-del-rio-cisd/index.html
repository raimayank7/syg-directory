San Felipe-Del Rio Cisd: public schools

14 schools

Texas, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in the San Felipe-Del Rio Cisd district in Texas: 8 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,907 students in 14 of them; the median school has 577 students and the largest has 2,491. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 127th largest of the 1,210 districts in Texas by number of public schools, and holds 0.15% of the state's 9,217 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Del Rio (13), Laughlin Afb (1).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 2 of the 14 schools offer prekindergarten and 8 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 8 to 8 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 1 to 2, and for grades 9 to 12 from 3 to 3. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 14 of the 14 schools of this district. The middle school has 577 students; a quarter of them have 466 or fewer and a quarter have 665 or more. The smallest count is 102 (Blended Academy) and the largest is 2,491 (Del Rio H S). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 2 ZIP codes, the most schools in 78840 (13). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
