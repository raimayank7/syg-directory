Midland Public Schools: public schools

13 schools

Michigan, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Midland Public Schools district in Michigan: 7 elementary, 2 middle, 2 high, 1 ungraded, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools. The file reports 7,359 students in 13 of them; the median school has 601 students and the largest has 1,265. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

This is the 34th largest of the 881 districts in Michigan by number of public schools, and holds 0.37% of the state's 3,485 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Midland.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 1 of the 13 schools offers prekindergarten and 7 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 6 to 7 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 3 to 3, and for grades 9 to 12 from 3 to 3. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 13 of the 13 schools of this district. The middle school has 601 students; a quarter of them have 393 or fewer and a quarter have 808 or more. The smallest count is 10 (Post Secondary Transition Program) and the largest is 1,265 (HH Dow High School). 3 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 2 ZIP codes, the most schools in 48640 (7). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
