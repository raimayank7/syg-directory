Charter Institute at Erskine: public schools

27 schools

South Carolina, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Charter Institute at Erskine district in South Carolina: 11 other, 8 high, 8 elementary. 27 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 25,146 students in 27 of them; the median school has 602 students and the largest has 5,955. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 12th largest of the 94 districts in South Carolina by number of public schools, and holds 2.13% of the state's 1,270 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 21 towns, most in West Columbia (2), Columbia (2), Greenville (2), Simpsonville (2).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 4 of the 27 schools offer prekindergarten and 19 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 19 to 19 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 18 to 22, and for grades 9 to 12 from 16 to 19. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 27 of the 27 schools of this district. The middle school has 602 students; a quarter of them have 371 or fewer and a quarter have 786 or more. The smallest count is 80 (Summit Classical School) and the largest is 5,955 (SC Connections Academy). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Charter Institute At Erskine (27). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 24 ZIP codes, the most schools in 29169 (2). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
