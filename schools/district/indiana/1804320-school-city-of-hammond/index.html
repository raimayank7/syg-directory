School City of Hammond: public schools

17 schools

Indiana, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in the School City of Hammond district in Indiana: 12 elementary, 3 high, 2 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 11,275 students in 17 of them; the median school has 626 students and the largest has 1,923. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 15th largest of the 426 districts in Indiana by number of public schools, and holds 0.89% of the state's 1,917 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Hammond (16), Whiting (1).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 12 of the 17 schools offer prekindergarten and 12 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 12 to 12 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 3 to 13, and for grades 9 to 12 from 3 to 3. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 17 of the 17 schools of this district. The middle school has 626 students; a quarter of them have 450 or fewer and a quarter have 734 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Area Career Center) and the largest is 1,923 (Hammond Central High School). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 5 ZIP codes, the most schools in 46324 (6). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
