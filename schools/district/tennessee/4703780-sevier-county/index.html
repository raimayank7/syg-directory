Sevier County: public schools

33 schools

Tennessee, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 33 public schools in the Sevier County district in Tennessee: 14 elementary, 9 middle, 8 high, 1 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 14,435 students in 33 of them; the median school has 455 students and the largest has 1,040. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 9th largest of the 148 districts in Tennessee by number of public schools, and holds 1.73% of the state's 1,905 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 6 towns, most in Sevierville (17), Kodak (4), Seymour (4), Gatlinburg (4).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 10 of the 33 schools offer prekindergarten and 13 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 12 to 13 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 8 to 15, and for grades 9 to 12 from 9 to 9. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 33 of the 33 schools of this district. The middle school has 455 students; a quarter of them have 301 or fewer and a quarter have 617 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Douglas Dam Head Start) and the largest is 1,040 (Sevier County Junior High). 6 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 7 ZIP codes, the most schools in 37862 (9). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
