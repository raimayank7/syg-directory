Raleigh County Schools: public schools

27 schools

West Virginia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 27 public schools in the Raleigh County Schools district in West Virginia: 17 elementary, 5 middle, 5 high. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 10,584 students in 26 of them; the median school has 366 students and the largest has 1,265. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 4th largest of the 61 districts in West Virginia by number of public schools, and holds 3.96% of the state's 682 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 12 towns, most in Beckley (11), Shady Spring (3), Coal City (2), Sophia (2).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 17 of the 27 schools offer prekindergarten and 17 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 17 to 17 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 5 to 6, and for grades 9 to 12 from 5 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 26 of the 27 schools of this district. The middle school has 366 students; a quarter of them have 246 or fewer and a quarter have 459 or more. The smallest count is 99 (Clear Fork District Elementary) and the largest is 1,265 (Woodrow Wilson High School). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. 1 school has no count in the file (new schools, schools that report no membership, or ungraded programmes). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 12 ZIP codes, the most schools in 25801 (11). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
