Palmdale Elementary: public schools

29 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 29 public schools in the Palmdale Elementary district in California: 23 elementary, 5 middle, 1 other. 1 of them (3.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 4 alternative schools, 2 special education schools. The file reports 17,935 students in 29 of them; the median school has 618 students and the largest has 1,025. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 53rd largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.28% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Palmdale.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 0 of the 29 schools offer prekindergarten and 24 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 24 to 24 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 14 to 14, and for grades 9 to 12 from 1 to 1. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 29 of the 29 schools of this district. The middle school has 618 students; a quarter of them have 494 or fewer and a quarter have 786 or more. The smallest count is 51 (Oak Tree Community Day) and the largest is 1,025 (Los Amigos - Dual Immersion). 2 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Palmdale Elementary (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 3 ZIP codes, the most schools in 93550 (14). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
