Greenfield Union: public schools

13 schools

California, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in the Greenfield Union district in California: 10 elementary, 3 middle. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 9,021 students in 13 of them; the median school has 725 students and the largest has 1,094. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 8.

This is the 185th largest of the 2,048 districts in California by number of public schools, and holds 0.13% of the state's 10,326 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Bakersfield.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 0 of the 13 schools offer prekindergarten and 10 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 10 to 10 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 4 to 4, and for grades 9 to 12 from 0 to 0. No school in this list offers grade 9, grade 10, grade 11 or grade 12. That can happen when a district sends those students to schools run by another district, to a state or county school, or to a charter school; the directory does not say which, so ask the district. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 13 of the 13 schools of this district. The middle school has 725 students; a quarter of them have 570 or fewer and a quarter have 849 or more. The smallest count is 12 (Greenfield Community) and the largest is 1,094 (Leon H. Ollivier Middle). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 3 ZIP codes, the most schools in 93307 (9). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
