Mapleton School District No. 1 in the county of Adams & St: public schools

20 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Mapleton School District No. 1 in the county of Adams & St district in Colorado: 10 elementary, 6 high, 2 other, 1 middle, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,016 students in 20 of them; the median school has 376 students and the largest has 767. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 22nd largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 1.05% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

Its schools are in 2 towns, most in Thornton (10), Denver (10).

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 9 of the 20 schools offer prekindergarten and 12 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 11 to 12 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 10 to 11, and for grades 9 to 12 from 8 to 8. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 20 of the 20 schools of this district. The middle school has 376 students; a quarter of them have 264 or fewer and a quarter have 427 or more. The smallest count is 94 (North Valley School for Young Adults) and the largest is 767 (York International). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 4 ZIP codes, the most schools in 80229 (10). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
