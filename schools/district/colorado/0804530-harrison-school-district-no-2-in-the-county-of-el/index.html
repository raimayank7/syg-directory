Harrison School District No. 2 in the county of El Paso an: public schools

28 schools

Colorado, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in the Harrison School District No. 2 in the county of El Paso an district in Colorado: 16 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 1 other. 9 of them (32.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 12,024 students in 28 of them; the median school has 338 students and the largest has 1,143. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 19th largest of the 186 districts in Colorado by number of public schools, and holds 1.47% of the state's 1,908 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Colorado Springs.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 11 of the 28 schools offer prekindergarten and 17 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 17 to 17 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 9 to 10, and for grades 9 to 12 from 7 to 7. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 28 of the 28 schools of this district. The middle school has 338 students; a quarter of them have 270 or fewer and a quarter have 551 or more. The smallest count is 85 (Career Readiness Academy) and the largest is 1,143 (Harrison High School). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Harrison School District No. 2 In The County Of El Paso An (9). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 3 ZIP codes, the most schools in 80916 (14). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
