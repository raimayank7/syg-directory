Decatur City: public schools

20 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 20 public schools in the Decatur City district in Alabama: 12 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle, 1 other, 1 secondary. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 8,665 students in 18 of them; the median school has 408 students and the largest has 1,055. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 14th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 1.31% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Decatur.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 10 of the 20 schools offer prekindergarten and 13 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 12 to 12 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 3 to 4, and for grades 9 to 12 from 5 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 18 of the 20 schools of this district. The middle school has 408 students; a quarter of them have 290 or fewer and a quarter have 702 or more. The smallest count is 38 (Decatur High Developmental) and the largest is 1,055 (Austin High School). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. 2 schools have no count in the file (new schools, schools that report no membership, or ungraded programmes). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 2 ZIP codes, the most schools in 35601 (16). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
