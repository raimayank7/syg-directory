Auburn City: public schools

14 schools

Alabama, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in the Auburn City district in Alabama: 10 elementary, 2 middle, 1 high, 1 secondary. The file reports 9,466 students in 14 of them; the median school has 454 students and the largest has 2,163. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 34th largest of the 152 districts in Alabama by number of public schools, and holds 0.92% of the state's 1,524 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Auburn.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 3 of the 14 schools offer prekindergarten and 5 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 5 to 5 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 1 to 1, and for grades 9 to 12 from 1 to 1. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 14 of the 14 schools of this district. The middle school has 454 students; a quarter of them have 408 or fewer and a quarter have 720 or more. The smallest count is 358 (Woodland Pines Elementary School) and the largest is 2,163 (Auburn High School). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 2 ZIP codes, the most schools in 36830 (12). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
