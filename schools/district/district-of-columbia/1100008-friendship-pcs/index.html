Friendship PCS: public schools

15 schools

District Of Columbia, school district

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in the Friendship PCS district in District Of Columbia: 7 elementary, 6 middle, 2 high. 15 of them (100.0%) are charter schools. The file reports 4,511 students in 15 of them; the median school has 273 students and the largest has 715. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

This is the 3rd largest of the 64 districts in District Of Columbia by number of public schools, and holds 6.12% of the state's 245 schools in this file.

All of its schools are in Washington.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for this district: 6 of the 15 schools offer prekindergarten and 7 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 7 to 7 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 7 to 7, and for grades 9 to 12 from 2 to 2. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 15 of the 15 schools of this district. The middle school has 273 students; a quarter of them have 218 or fewer and a quarter have 327 or more. The smallest count is 172 (Friendship PCS - Ideal Middle) and the largest is 715 (Friendship PCS - Collegiate Academy). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Dc Public Charter School Board (15). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.

Where the schools are

The schools' street addresses fall in 7 ZIP codes, the most schools in 20032 (3). This is where the buildings stand, which is not always the same as the area the district serves.
