Public schools in Jackson, Tennessee

25 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 25 public schools in Jackson, Tennessee: 11 elementary, 6 high, 5 middle, 2 other, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 11,825 students in 25 of them; the median school has 470 students and the largest has 925. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Madison County (24), West Tennessee School for the Deaf (1).

Jackson is the 8th largest town in Tennessee by number of public schools in this file, out of 381 towns, and holds 1.31% of the state's 1,905 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Jackson: 10 of the 25 schools offer prekindergarten and 13 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 13 to 13 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 9 to 11, and for grades 9 to 12 from 8 to 8. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 25 of the 25 schools of Jackson. The middle school has 470 students; a quarter of them have 373 or fewer and a quarter have 613 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Parkview Prep Academy) and the largest is 925 (North Side High School). 2 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.
