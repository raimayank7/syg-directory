Public schools in Rio Rancho, New Mexico

24 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in Rio Rancho, New Mexico: 14 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 1 prekindergarten. 3 of them (12.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 17,684 students in 24 of them; the median school has 630 students and the largest has 2,625. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Rio Rancho (21), The Ask Academy (1), Sandoval Academy Of Bilingual Education (1), Explore Academy - Rio Rancho (1).

Rio Rancho is the 4th largest town in New Mexico by number of public schools in this file, out of 158 towns, and holds 2.70% of the state's 888 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Rio Rancho: 2 of the 24 schools offer prekindergarten and 14 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 13 to 14 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 7 to 8, and for grades 9 to 12 from 5 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 24 of the 24 schools of Rio Rancho. The middle school has 630 students; a quarter of them have 523 or fewer and a quarter have 800 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Sparrk Online Academy) and the largest is 2,625 (Cleveland High School). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Public Education Commission (3). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
