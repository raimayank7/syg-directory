Public schools in The Woodlands, Texas

24 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 24 public schools in The Woodlands, Texas: 16 elementary, 6 middle, 2 high. 2 of them (8.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools. The file reports 27,140 students in 24 of them; the median school has 843 students and the largest has 4,498. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Conroe Isd (18), Tomball Isd (4), School Of Science And Technology (1), Thrive Center For Success (1).

The Woodlands is the 65th largest town in Texas by number of public schools in this file, out of 1,088 towns, and holds 0.26% of the state's 9,217 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for The Woodlands: 14 of the 24 schools offer prekindergarten and 16 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 10 to 16 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 4 to 8, and for grades 9 to 12 from 2 to 2. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 24 of the 24 schools of The Woodlands. The middle school has 843 students; a quarter of them have 692 or fewer and a quarter have 1,229 or more. The smallest count is 127 (Thrive Center For Success) and the largest is 4,498 (The Woodlands H S). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: State Board Of Education (1), Commissioner Of Education (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
