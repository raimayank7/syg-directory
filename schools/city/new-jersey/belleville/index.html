Public schools in Belleville, New Jersey

10 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 10 public schools in Belleville, New Jersey: 7 elementary, 1 high, 1 middle, 1 prekindergarten. The file reports 4,962 students in 10 of them; the median school has 402 students and the largest has 1,591. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Belleville Public School District (10).

Belleville is the 45th largest town in New Jersey by number of public schools in this file, out of 572 towns, and holds 0.39% of the state's 2,558 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Belleville: 5 of the 10 schools offer prekindergarten and 7 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 7 to 7 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 1 to 7, and for grades 9 to 12 from 1 to 1. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 10 of the 10 schools of Belleville. The middle school has 402 students; a quarter of them have 250 or fewer and a quarter have 500 or more. The smallest count is 148 (Belleville PS9) and the largest is 1,591 (Belleville High School). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.
