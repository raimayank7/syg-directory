Public schools in Westfield, New Jersey

15 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 15 public schools in Westfield, New Jersey: 8 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 6,126 students in 15 of them; the median school has 309 students and the largest has 1,784. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Westfield Public School District (10), Union County Educational Services Commission (5).

Westfield is the 21st largest town in New Jersey by number of public schools in this file, out of 572 towns, and holds 0.59% of the state's 2,558 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Westfield: 2 of the 15 schools offer prekindergarten and 2 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 7 to 8 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 5 to 5, and for grades 9 to 12 from 4 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 15 of the 15 schools of Westfield. The middle school has 309 students; a quarter of them have 53 or fewer and a quarter have 531 or more. The smallest count is 26 (Work Readiness Academy) and the largest is 1,784 (Westfield Senior High School). 5 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.
