Public schools in Huntsville, Alabama

52 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 52 public schools in Huntsville, Alabama: 28 elementary, 12 middle, 9 high, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 2 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 26,137 students in 48 of them; the median school has 456 students and the largest has 1,885. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Huntsville City (42), Madison County (9), Al Inst Deaf And Blind (1).

Huntsville is the 4th largest town in Alabama by number of public schools in this file, out of 378 towns, and holds 3.41% of the state's 1,524 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Huntsville: 28 of the 52 schools offer prekindergarten and 29 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 29 to 30 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 15 to 24, and for grades 9 to 12 from 11 to 11. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 48 of the 52 schools of Huntsville. The middle school has 456 students; a quarter of them have 380 or fewer and a quarter have 584 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Madison County Career Technical Center) and the largest is 1,885 (Virgil Grissom High School). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. 4 schools have no count in the file (new schools, schools that report no membership, or ungraded programmes). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.
