Public schools in Bessemer, Alabama

16 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 16 public schools in Bessemer, Alabama: 9 elementary, 4 high, 1 secondary, 1 middle. 2 of them (12.5%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 special education schools, 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 6,068 students in 13 of them; the median school has 405 students and the largest has 805. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Bessemer City (9), Jefferson County (4), JF Ingram State Technical College (1), Alabama Aerospace and Aviation (1), Empower Community School (1).

Bessemer is the 13th largest town in Alabama by number of public schools in this file, out of 378 towns, and holds 1.05% of the state's 1,524 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Bessemer: 6 of the 16 schools offer prekindergarten and 9 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 8 to 9 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 3 to 4, and for grades 9 to 12 from 4 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 13 of the 16 schools of Bessemer. The middle school has 405 students; a quarter of them have 297 or fewer and a quarter have 648 or more. The smallest count is 168 (Alabama Aerospace and Aviation High School) and the largest is 805 (Bessemer City High School). 3 schools have no count in the file (new schools, schools that report no membership, or ungraded programmes). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Al Charter School Commission (2). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
