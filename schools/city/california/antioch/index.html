Public schools in Antioch, California

28 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 28 public schools in Antioch, California: 17 elementary, 5 high, 4 middle, 2 other. 3 of them (10.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 15,257 students in 28 of them; the median school has 451 students and the largest has 1,823. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: Antioch Unified (24), Antioch Charter Academy II District (1), Antioch Charter Academy District (1), Rocketship Delta Prep District (1), Contra Costa County Office of Education (1).

Antioch is the 69th largest town in California by number of public schools in this file, out of 1,010 towns, and holds 0.27% of the state's 10,326 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Antioch: 0 of the 28 schools offer prekindergarten and 19 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 19 to 19 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 9 to 11, and for grades 9 to 12 from 5 to 7. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 28 of the 28 schools of Antioch. The middle school has 451 students; a quarter of them have 343 or fewer and a quarter have 640 or more. The smallest count is 112 (CCCOE Special Education Programs) and the largest is 1,823 (Antioch High). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Antioch Charter Academy Ii District (1), Antioch Charter Academy District (1), Rocketship Delta Prep District (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
