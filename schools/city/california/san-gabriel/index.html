Public schools in San Gabriel, California

13 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 13 public schools in San Gabriel, California: 7 elementary, 4 high, 1 middle. 1 of them (7.7%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools. The file reports 7,591 students in 12 of them; the median school has 424 students and the largest has 1,768. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 5 school districts: San Gabriel Unified (8), Garvey Elementary (2), Alhambra Unified (1), Options for Youth San Gabriel District (1), Temple City Unified (1).

San Gabriel is the 204th largest town in California by number of public schools in this file, out of 1,010 towns, and holds 0.13% of the state's 10,326 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for San Gabriel: 0 of the 13 schools offer prekindergarten and 7 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 7 to 7 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 2 to 3, and for grades 9 to 12 from 3 to 4. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 12 of the 13 schools of San Gabriel. The middle school has 424 students; a quarter of them have 361 or fewer and a quarter have 959 or more. The smallest count is 46 (Del Mar High) and the largest is 1,768 (San Gabriel High). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. 1 school has no count in the file (new schools, schools that report no membership, or ungraded programmes). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Options For Youth San Gabriel District (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
