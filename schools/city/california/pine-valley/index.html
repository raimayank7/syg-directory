Public schools in Pine Valley, California

7 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 7 public schools in Pine Valley, California: 4 high, 1 other, 1 middle. 1 of them (14.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 928 students in 6 of them; the median school has 95 students and the largest has 422. Grades offered run from kindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Mountain Empire Unified (6), JCS - Pine Valley District (1).

Pine Valley is the 363rd largest town in California by number of public schools in this file, out of 1,010 towns, and holds 0.07% of the state's 10,326 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Pine Valley: 0 of the 7 schools offer prekindergarten and 1 offers kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 1 to 1 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 1 to 4, and for grades 9 to 12 from 5 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 6 of the 7 schools of Pine Valley. The middle school has 95 students; a quarter of them have 17 or fewer and a quarter have 298 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Cottonwood Community Day) and the largest is 422 (Mountain Empire High). 3 schools have fewer than 100 students. 1 school has no count in the file (new schools, schools that report no membership, or ungraded programmes). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Jcs - Pine Valley District (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
