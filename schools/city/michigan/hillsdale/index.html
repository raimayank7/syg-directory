Public schools in Hillsdale, Michigan

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Hillsdale, Michigan: 3 elementary, 3 high, 2 other, 1 middle. 2 of them (22.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 1,631 students in 9 of them; the median school has 54 students and the largest has 436. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Hillsdale Community Schools (5), Hillsdale ISD (2), Hillsdale Preparatory School (1), Will Carleton Charter School Academy (1).

Hillsdale is the 89th largest town in Michigan by number of public schools in this file, out of 597 towns, and holds 0.26% of the state's 3,485 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Hillsdale: 2 of the 9 schools offer prekindergarten and 5 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 4 to 4 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 4 to 4, and for grades 9 to 12 from 4 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 9 of the 9 schools of Hillsdale. The middle school has 54 students; a quarter of them have 36 or fewer and a quarter have 368 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Hillsdale Area Career Center) and the largest is 436 (Gier Elementary School). 5 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Grand Valley State University (1), Hillsdale Isd (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
