Public schools in Bloomfield Hills, Michigan

12 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 12 public schools in Bloomfield Hills, Michigan: 4 elementary, 3 middle, 2 other, 2 high, 1 prekindergarten. Besides regular schools there are 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 6,742 students in 12 of them; the median school has 516 students and the largest has 1,567. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Bloomfield Hills Schools (8), Birmingham Public Schools (3), Oakland Schools (1).

Bloomfield Hills is the 62nd largest town in Michigan by number of public schools in this file, out of 597 towns, and holds 0.34% of the state's 3,485 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Bloomfield Hills: 3 of the 12 schools offer prekindergarten and 6 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 6 to 9 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 5 to 5, and for grades 9 to 12 from 4 to 4. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 12 of the 12 schools of Bloomfield Hills. The middle school has 516 students; a quarter of them have 384 or fewer and a quarter have 639 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Charles L Bowers Farm) and the largest is 1,567 (Bloomfield Hills High School). 3 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.
