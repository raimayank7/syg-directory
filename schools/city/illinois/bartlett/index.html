Public schools in Bartlett, Illinois

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Bartlett, Illinois: 6 elementary, 1 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 high. The file reports 5,914 students in 9 of them; the median school has 533 students and the largest has 2,091. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Sd U-46 (9).

Bartlett is the 113th largest town in Illinois by number of public schools in this file, out of 861 towns, and holds 0.20% of the state's 4,400 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Bartlett: 5 of the 9 schools offer prekindergarten and 6 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 6 to 6 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 1 to 6, and for grades 9 to 12 from 1 to 1. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 9 of the 9 schools of Bartlett. The middle school has 533 students; a quarter of them have 426 or fewer and a quarter have 660 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Independence Preschool) and the largest is 2,091 (Bartlett High School). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.
