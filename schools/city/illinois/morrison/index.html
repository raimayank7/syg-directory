Public schools in Morrison, Illinois

9 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 9 public schools in Morrison, Illinois: 2 high, 2 elementary, 1 middle. Besides regular schools there are 4 special education schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 985 students in 5 of them; the median school has 218 students and the largest has 290. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Bi-County Special Educ Coop (4), Morrison CUSD 6 (4), Lee/Ogle/Whiteside ROE (1).

Morrison is the 101st largest town in Illinois by number of public schools in this file, out of 861 towns, and holds 0.20% of the state's 4,400 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Morrison: 1 of the 9 schools offers prekindergarten and 1 offers kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 1 to 1 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 1 to 1, and for grades 9 to 12 from 2 to 2. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 5 of the 9 schools of Morrison. The middle school has 218 students; a quarter of them have 214 or fewer and a quarter have 263 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Morrison Alt Learning Program) and the largest is 290 (Morrison High School). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. 4 schools have no count in the file (new schools, schools that report no membership, or ungraded programmes). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.
