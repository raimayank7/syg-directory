Public schools in Oswego, Illinois

14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in Oswego, Illinois: 6 elementary, 3 high, 3 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools. The file reports 11,475 students in 14 of them; the median school has 526 students and the largest has 2,866. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Cusd 308 (13), Grundy/Kendall ROE (1).

Oswego is the 43rd largest town in Illinois by number of public schools in this file, out of 861 towns, and holds 0.32% of the state's 4,400 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Oswego: 1 of the 14 schools offers prekindergarten and 7 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 7 to 7 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 5 to 5, and for grades 9 to 12 from 4 to 4. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 14 of the 14 schools of Oswego. The middle school has 526 students; a quarter of them have 376 or fewer and a quarter have 837 or more. The smallest count is 0 (East View Academy School) and the largest is 2,866 (Oswego High School). 2 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.
