Public schools in San Tan Valley, Arizona

18 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 18 public schools in San Tan Valley, Arizona: 13 elementary, 4 high, 1 other. 6 of them (33.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 12,383 students in 18 of them; the median school has 591 students and the largest has 2,168. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 8 school districts: Florence Unified School District (4437) (6), J O Combs Unified School District (4445) (5), American Leadership Academy Inc. (4348) (2), Fit Kids Inc. dba Champion Schools (78783) (1), Happy Valley East (90906) (1), American Charter Schools Foundation dba Ridgeview (1001346) (1), East Valley Institute of Technology (4516) (1), Legacy Traditional School-San Tan (1001927) (1).

San Tan Valley is the 22nd largest town in Arizona by number of public schools in this file, out of 201 towns, and holds 0.74% of the state's 2,427 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for San Tan Valley: 6 of the 18 schools offer prekindergarten and 14 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 14 to 14 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 10 to 14, and for grades 9 to 12 from 5 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 18 of the 18 schools of San Tan Valley. The middle school has 591 students; a quarter of them have 424 or fewer and a quarter have 742 or more. The smallest count is 1 (EVIT - Combs High School) and the largest is 2,168 (American Leadership Academy Ironwood K12). 1 school has fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Charter Board (Csb) (6). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
