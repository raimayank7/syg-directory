Public schools in Charlottesville, Virginia

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Charlottesville, Virginia: 15 elementary, 6 middle, 4 high, 1 other. 1 of them (3.2%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 career and technical schools, 2 special education schools, 2 alternative schools. The file reports 14,277 students in 25 of them; the median school has 488 students and the largest has 2,005. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 6 school districts: Albemarle County Public Schools (17), Charlottesville City Public Schools (10), Charlottesville-Albemarle Vocational-Technical Center (1), Piedmont Regional Education Program (1), University of Virginia (1), Department of Education State-Operated Educ Prgms (1).

Charlottesville is the 13th largest town in Virginia by number of public schools in this file, out of 383 towns, and holds 1.46% of the state's 2,129 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Charlottesville: 15 of the 31 schools offer prekindergarten and 16 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 11 to 16 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 7 to 7, and for grades 9 to 12 from 5 to 5. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 25 of the 31 schools of Charlottesville. The middle school has 488 students; a quarter of them have 328 or fewer and a quarter have 604 or more. The smallest count is 179 (Community Lab School) and the largest is 2,005 (Albemarle High). 6 schools have no count in the file (new schools, schools that report no membership, or ungraded programmes). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Albemarle County Public Schools (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
