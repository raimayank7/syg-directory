Public schools in Bellevue, Washington

31 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 31 public schools in Bellevue, Washington: 17 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 2 other. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools. The file reports 20,299 students in 31 of them; the median school has 555 students and the largest has 1,844. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 2 school districts: Bellevue School District (29), Issaquah School District (2).

Bellevue is the 16th largest town in Washington by number of public schools in this file, out of 350 towns, and holds 1.21% of the state's 2,553 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Bellevue: 14 of the 31 schools offer prekindergarten and 19 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 19 to 19 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 9 to 9, and for grades 9 to 12 from 8 to 9. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 31 of the 31 schools of Bellevue. The middle school has 555 students; a quarter of them have 444 or fewer and a quarter have 714 or more. The smallest count is 27 (Bellevue Open Doors Reengagement) and the largest is 1,844 (Newport Senior High School). 2 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.
