Public schools in Bremerton, Washington

23 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 23 public schools in Bremerton, Washington: 13 elementary, 6 high, 2 middle, 1 prekindergarten, 1 other. 1 of them (4.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 3 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools, 1 special education schools. The file reports 9,196 students in 23 of them; the median school has 356 students and the largest has 1,279. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 4 school districts: Bremerton School District (13), Central Kitsap School District (8), Washington Military Department (1), Catalyst Public Schools (1).

Bremerton is the 24th largest town in Washington by number of public schools in this file, out of 350 towns, and holds 0.90% of the state's 2,553 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Bremerton: 9 of the 23 schools offer prekindergarten and 14 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 14 to 14 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 5 to 5, and for grades 9 to 12 from 5 to 7. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 23 of the 23 schools of Bremerton. The middle school has 356 students; a quarter of them have 140 or fewer and a quarter have 487 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Morgan Center School) and the largest is 1,279 (Bremerton High School). 4 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Washington State Charter School Commission (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
