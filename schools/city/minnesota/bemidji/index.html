Public schools in Bemidji, Minnesota

22 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 22 public schools in Bemidji, Minnesota: 7 elementary, 7 high, 4 middle, 2 other, 2 prekindergarten. 6 of them (27.3%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 alternative schools, 2 special education schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 5,393 students in 22 of them; the median school has 99 students and the largest has 1,516. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Bemidji Public School District (14), Treknorth High School (2), Voyageurs Expeditionary (2), North Country Voc. Coop. Ctr. (1), Bemidji Regional Interdist. Council (1), Schoolcraft Learning Community Chtr (1), Aurora Waasakone Community of Learn (1).

Bemidji is the 13th largest town in Minnesota by number of public schools in this file, out of 467 towns, and holds 0.82% of the state's 2,696 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Bemidji: 6 of the 22 schools offer prekindergarten and 7 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 5 to 8 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 10 to 11, and for grades 9 to 12 from 9 to 9. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 22 of the 22 schools of Bemidji. The middle school has 99 students; a quarter of them have 42 or fewer and a quarter have 285 or more. The smallest count is 0 (North Country Voc. Coop. Ctr.) and the largest is 1,516 (Bemidji Senior High). 11 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Volunteers Of America - Minnesota (3), Osprey Wilds Environmental Learning Center (3). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
