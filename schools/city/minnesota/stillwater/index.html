Public schools in Stillwater, Minnesota

21 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 21 public schools in Stillwater, Minnesota: 9 elementary, 7 high, 2 middle, 2 prekindergarten, 1 other. 6 of them (28.6%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 5 special education schools, 3 alternative schools. The file reports 7,509 students in 21 of them; the median school has 126 students and the largest has 2,713. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 7 school districts: Stillwater Area Public Schools (11), St. Croix Preparatory Academy (3), Mahtomedi Public School District (2), New Heights School Inc. (2), Minnesota Department Of Corrections (1), Marine Area Community School (1), Northeast Metro 916 (1).

Stillwater is the 14th largest town in Minnesota by number of public schools in this file, out of 467 towns, and holds 0.78% of the state's 2,696 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Stillwater: 4 of the 21 schools offer prekindergarten and 9 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 7 to 9 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 5 to 6, and for grades 9 to 12 from 7 to 8. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 21 of the 21 schools of Stillwater. The middle school has 126 students; a quarter of them have 30 or fewer and a quarter have 432 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Mcf - Oak Park Heights) and the largest is 2,713 (Stillwater Area High School). 10 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Friends Of Education (3), Minnesota Guild Of Public Charter Schools (3). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
