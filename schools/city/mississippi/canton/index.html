Public schools in Canton, Mississippi

14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in Canton, Mississippi: 7 elementary, 3 middle, 2 high, 1 other, 1 secondary. 1 of them (7.1%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 1 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 4,272 students in 14 of them; the median school has 381 students and the largest has 749. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Canton Public School Dist (10), Madison Co School Dist (3), Sr1 College Prep & Stem Academy (1).

Canton is the 12th largest town in Mississippi by number of public schools in this file, out of 239 towns, and holds 1.36% of the state's 1,032 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Canton: 5 of the 14 schools offer prekindergarten and 7 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 6 to 7 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 4 to 4, and for grades 9 to 12 from 2 to 3. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 14 of the 14 schools of Canton. The middle school has 381 students; a quarter of them have 139 or fewer and a quarter have 391 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Canton Career Center) and the largest is 749 (Madison Crossing Elementary). 3 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Mississippi Charter School Authorizer Board (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
