Public schools in Frederick, Maryland

32 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 32 public schools in Frederick, Maryland: 19 elementary, 7 high, 5 middle, 1 other. 3 of them (9.4%) are charter schools. Besides regular schools there are 2 alternative schools, 1 career and technical schools. The file reports 22,604 students in 32 of them; the median school has 622 students and the largest has 2,031. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 1 school district: Frederick County Public Schools (32).

Frederick is the 4th largest town in Maryland by number of public schools in this file, out of 258 towns, and holds 2.27% of the state's 1,408 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Frederick: 18 of the 32 schools offer prekindergarten and 20 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 20 to 20 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 9 to 9, and for grades 9 to 12 from 7 to 8. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 32 of the 32 schools of Frederick. The middle school has 622 students; a quarter of them have 468 or fewer and a quarter have 792 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Frederick County Career & Technology Center) and the largest is 2,031 (Gov. Thomas Johnson High). 3 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Frederick County Public Schools (3). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
