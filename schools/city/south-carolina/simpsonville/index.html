Public schools in Simpsonville, South Carolina

17 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 17 public schools in Simpsonville, South Carolina: 10 elementary, 4 middle, 3 high. 3 of them (17.6%) are charter schools. The file reports 15,918 students in 17 of them; the median school has 909 students and the largest has 2,085. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to grade 12.

They belong to 3 school districts: Greenville 01 (14), Charter Institute at Erskine (2), SC Public Charter School District (1).

Simpsonville is the 13th largest town in South Carolina by number of public schools in this file, out of 243 towns, and holds 1.34% of the state's 1,270 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Simpsonville: 5 of the 17 schools offer prekindergarten and 10 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 10 to 10 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 5 to 6, and for grades 9 to 12 from 3 to 3. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 17 of the 17 schools of Simpsonville. The middle school has 909 students; a quarter of them have 774 or fewer and a quarter have 966 or more. The smallest count is 331 (GREEN Upstate High School Greenville) and the largest is 2,085 (Hillcrest High). Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.

Who authorises the charter schools

For the charter schools in this list the directory names the body that granted the charter: Charter Institute At Erskine (2), Sc Public Charter School District (1). A charter school is publicly funded and free to attend, but it is run under an agreement with its authoriser instead of directly by a district board.
