Public schools in Lincolnton, North Carolina

14 schools

The Common Core of Data 2023-24 lists 14 public schools in Lincolnton, North Carolina: 7 elementary, 4 high, 2 middle, 1 ungraded. Besides regular schools there are 1 career and technical schools, 1 alternative schools. The file reports 6,174 students in 14 of them; the median school has 388 students and the largest has 1,139. Grades offered run from prekindergarten to ungraded.

They belong to 1 school district: Lincoln County Schools (14).

Lincolnton is the 38th largest town in North Carolina by number of public schools in this file, out of 498 towns, and holds 0.52% of the state's 2,716 schools.

Which grades the schools offer

The directory marks, for each school, every grade it offers. Counting those marks for Lincolnton: 6 of the 14 schools offer prekindergarten and 6 offer kindergarten. For grades 1 to 5 the counts run from 6 to 6 schools per grade, for grades 6 to 8 from 3 to 3, and for grades 9 to 12 from 4 to 4. Every grade from 1 to 12 is offered by at least one school in the list. A school that offers a wide span, such as kindergarten to grade 12, is counted in every grade it covers, so the counts add up to more than the number of schools. The counts come from the directory's yes and no marks and describe what schools are listed as offering, not how many seats are open.

How large the schools are

The membership file gives a student count for 14 of the 14 schools of Lincolnton. The middle school has 388 students; a quarter of them have 291 or fewer and a quarter have 563 or more. The smallest count is 0 (Lcst) and the largest is 1,139 (North Lincoln High). 2 schools have fewer than 100 students. Counts are the total membership of each school in the 2023-24 school year as the file reports it, with adult education left out. They describe size, and nothing about the quality of teaching or results.
