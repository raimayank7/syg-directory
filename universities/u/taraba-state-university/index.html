Taraba State University

Nigeria

Taraba State University is a university in Nigeria. Wikidata gives its year of founding as 2008. The number of students recorded is 8,165 (the year of this count is not shown here and may be old). Its website is tsuniversity.edu.ng.

How it compares inside Nigeria

Of the 322 universities in Nigeria with a recorded founding year, this one is the 133rd oldest by that year (a plain sort of the years, not a claim about which is the oldest). The median founding year among them is 2011. Among the 95 universities in Nigeria with a recorded student number, it is 41st by that number (the counts are of different years and definitions, so treat the order as rough). The median recorded number is 6,061.

The founding year in context

The founding year 2008 falls in the 21st century. 239 of the 322 universities listed for Nigeria with a founding year (74%) have a year in that same century. The nearest entries before and after it in the list sorted by recorded year are Olusegun Agagu University of Science and Technology (2008) and Afe Babalola University Talent Discovery Centre (2009). Years in Wikidata are taken as entered: some give the year a predecessor school opened, some the year a university charter or status began.

The size figure

8,165 students is about 1.3 times the middle recorded value for Nigeria (6,061). 40 of the 95 universities in Nigeria with a recorded number have a larger figure and 54 have a smaller one. Wikidata does not say which year a count belongs to, whether part-time or distance students are included, or whether the figure is for the whole university or one campus, so use it as a rough size band only.
