Charitable, educational, religious and similar organization (501(c)(3))

1,656,344 organizations

IRS subsection code 03: 1,656,344 organizations in the file (83.9% of the 1,975,209 counted here). The states with the most: California (179,161), Texas (134,559), New York (107,389), Florida (104,061), Pennsylvania (65,579), Ohio (61,535), Illinois (58,950), Georgia (56,601).

Fields of work inside this type

The organizations of this type are coded as follows by field of work: 347,756 no code, and among the coded ones religion-related (206,442), education (185,126), human services (155,884), arts, culture and humanities (119,079), philanthropy, voluntarism and grantmaking foundations (104,905), recreation and sports (99,803). The type tells you under which subsection of section 501(c) the IRS recognised the exemption; the field tells you roughly what the organization does, so the two views complement each other.

How large they report themselves to be

All 1,656,344 organizations of this type carry an income code from the latest return. Of those, 4% report income under $25,000, 19% report $25,000 to $499,999, 9% report $500,000 to $4,999,999 and 3% report $5 million or more; 65% have income code 0, which the IRS table lists as $0; that covers organizations that reported no income and probably also organizations that have no return data in the file, so the file alone cannot tell the two apart. The bands are the IRS's own income codes for the most recent return, and a return can be a few years old. The IRS information sheet says amounts are missing for organizations that file only the small e-Postcard or that are not required to file.

Gifts, legal form and group ties

The file marks contributions as deductible for 1,636,596 of the organizations (99%) and as not deductible for 3,190 (0%). By type of organization in the file: 1,372,962 corporations, 236,487 associations, 41,118 trusts, 674 co-operatives. 1,412,697 are independent organizations and 237,259 are subordinate units in a group ruling, such as a local branch of a larger body.

The foundation code, which the IRS uses for 501(c)(3) organizations, marks 1,176,098 organizations that get a substantial part of their support from the public or government, 293,146 churches, 129,812 private foundations, 24,471 schools, 23,223 supporting organizations. These are the IRS's own classes and the file does not describe an organization's work.

When the exemption was recognised

The ruling date is the month and year of the IRS letter that recognised the exemption, which is not the year the organization was founded. 741,183 of the 1,656,344 organizations with a ruling date (45%) were recognised in 2016 or later. The decades with the most rulings are the 2020s (558,370), the 2010s (391,537), the 2000s (220,152).

By filing requirement code: 399,805 must file Form 990 or 990-EZ, 822,624 need only file the small Form 990-N (income under $50,000 a year, in the IRS's words), and 433,500 are marked as not required to file (churches, religious organizations, government bodies and others).

Data: IRS Exempt Organizations Business Master File (posted 10 November 2026), read 14 November 2026. Counts and amounts only, no rating or recommendation, no street addresses. Independent site, not connected with the IRS or any charity.
