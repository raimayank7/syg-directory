Tax-exempt organizations in Santa Barbara, California

1,404 organizations

The IRS file has 1,404 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Santa Barbara, California.

What kinds of organizations

By field of work, 366 have no code and the coded ones are mostly arts, culture and humanities (149), philanthropy, voluntarism and grantmaking foundations (146), education (111), human services (85), religion-related (78), recreation and sports (75), environment (53). By type of exemption the largest groups are charitable, educational, religious and similar organization (1,259), business league, chamber of commerce and board of trade (40), social and recreational club (37). The share of Santa Barbara in the state's 204,463 organizations is 1%.

How large they report themselves to be

All 1,404 organizations in Santa Barbara carry an income code from the latest return. Of those, 5% report income under $25,000, 26% report $25,000 to $499,999, 14% report $500,000 to $4,999,999 and 8% report $5 million or more; 47% have income code 0, which the IRS table lists as $0; that covers organizations that reported no income and probably also organizations that have no return data in the file, so the file alone cannot tell the two apart. The bands are the IRS's own income codes for the most recent return, and a return can be a few years old. The IRS information sheet says amounts are missing for organizations that file only the small e-Postcard or that are not required to file.

Gifts, legal form and group ties

The file marks contributions as deductible for 1,268 of the organizations (90%) and as not deductible for 125 (9%). By type of organization in the file: 1,149 corporations, 189 associations, 59 trusts, 2 co-operatives. 1,228 are independent organizations and 170 are subordinate units in a group ruling, such as a local branch of a larger body.

The foundation code, which the IRS uses for 501(c)(3) organizations, marks 885 organizations that get a substantial part of their support from the public or government, 192 private foundations, 133 churches, 22 schools, 15 supporting organizations. These are the IRS's own classes and the file does not describe an organization's work.

When the exemption was recognised

The ruling date is the month and year of the IRS letter that recognised the exemption, which is not the year the organization was founded. 439 of the 1,404 organizations with a ruling date (31%) were recognised in 2016 or later. The decades with the most rulings are the 2020s (308), the 2010s (285), the 2000s (214).

By filing requirement code: 506 must file Form 990 or 990-EZ, 557 need only file the small Form 990-N (income under $50,000 a year, in the IRS's words), and 340 are marked as not required to file (churches, religious organizations, government bodies and others).
