Tax-exempt organizations in Chesapeake, Virginia

1,293 organizations

The IRS file has 1,293 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Chesapeake, Virginia.

What kinds of organizations

By field of work, 328 have no code and the coded ones are mostly religion-related (216), human services (130), education (130), recreation and sports (80), youth development (62), arts, culture and humanities (55), community improvement and capacity building (42). By type of exemption the largest groups are charitable, educational, religious and similar organization (1,104), fraternal beneficiary society (47), civic league and social welfare organization (34). The share of Chesapeake in the state's 54,761 organizations is 2%.

How large they report themselves to be

All 1,293 organizations in Chesapeake carry an income code from the latest return. Of those, 4% report income under $25,000, 14% report $25,000 to $499,999, 4% report $500,000 to $4,999,999 and 1% report $5 million or more; 77% have income code 0, which the IRS table lists as $0; that covers organizations that reported no income and probably also organizations that have no return data in the file, so the file alone cannot tell the two apart. The bands are the IRS's own income codes for the most recent return, and a return can be a few years old. The IRS information sheet says amounts are missing for organizations that file only the small e-Postcard or that are not required to file.

Gifts, legal form and group ties

The file marks contributions as deductible for 1,160 of the organizations (90%) and as not deductible for 117 (9%). By type of organization in the file: 979 corporations, 299 associations, 5 trusts. 1,002 are independent organizations and 288 are subordinate units in a group ruling, such as a local branch of a larger body.

The foundation code, which the IRS uses for 501(c)(3) organizations, marks 819 organizations that get a substantial part of their support from the public or government, 216 churches, 46 private foundations, 12 schools, 5 supporting organizations. These are the IRS's own classes and the file does not describe an organization's work.

When the exemption was recognised

The ruling date is the month and year of the IRS letter that recognised the exemption, which is not the year the organization was founded. 636 of the 1,293 organizations with a ruling date (49%) were recognised in 2016 or later. The decades with the most rulings are the 2020s (511), the 2010s (260), the 2000s (154).

By filing requirement code: 220 must file Form 990 or 990-EZ, 805 need only file the small Form 990-N (income under $50,000 a year, in the IRS's words), and 267 are marked as not required to file (churches, religious organizations, government bodies and others).
