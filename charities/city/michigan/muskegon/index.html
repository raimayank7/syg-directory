Tax-exempt organizations in Muskegon, Michigan

435 organizations

The IRS file has 435 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Muskegon, Michigan.

What kinds of organizations

By field of work, 151 have no code and the coded ones are mostly religion-related (44), human services (34), recreation and sports (33), arts, culture and humanities (27), education (25), community improvement and capacity building (19), youth development (17). By type of exemption the largest groups are charitable, educational, religious and similar organization (364), civic league and social welfare organization (18), agricultural, horticultural and labor organization (14). The share of Muskegon in the state's 57,026 organizations is 1%.

How large they report themselves to be

All 435 organizations in Muskegon carry an income code from the latest return. Of those, 1% report income under $25,000, 18% report $25,000 to $499,999, 11% report $500,000 to $4,999,999 and 3% report $5 million or more; 66% have income code 0, which the IRS table lists as $0; that covers organizations that reported no income and probably also organizations that have no return data in the file, so the file alone cannot tell the two apart. The bands are the IRS's own income codes for the most recent return, and a return can be a few years old. The IRS information sheet says amounts are missing for organizations that file only the small e-Postcard or that are not required to file.

Gifts, legal form and group ties

The file marks contributions as deductible for 371 of the organizations (85%) and as not deductible for 51 (12%). By type of organization in the file: 335 corporations, 95 associations, 1 trusts. 331 are independent organizations and 101 are subordinate units in a group ruling, such as a local branch of a larger body.

The foundation code, which the IRS uses for 501(c)(3) organizations, marks 252 organizations that get a substantial part of their support from the public or government, 85 churches, 13 supporting organizations, 10 private foundations, 2 hospitals or medical research organizations. These are the IRS's own classes and the file does not describe an organization's work.

When the exemption was recognised

The ruling date is the month and year of the IRS letter that recognised the exemption, which is not the year the organization was founded. 138 of the 435 organizations with a ruling date (32%) were recognised in 2016 or later. The decades with the most rulings are the 2020s (107), the 2010s (94), the 1980s (46).

By filing requirement code: 130 must file Form 990 or 990-EZ, 207 need only file the small Form 990-N (income under $50,000 a year, in the IRS's words), and 96 are marked as not required to file (churches, religious organizations, government bodies and others).
