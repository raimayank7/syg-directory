Tax-exempt organizations in Carmel, Indiana

1,671 organizations

The IRS file has 1,671 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Carmel, Indiana.

What kinds of organizations

By field of work, 984 have no code and the coded ones are mostly education (126), philanthropy, voluntarism and grantmaking foundations (90), religion-related (67), recreation and sports (61), arts, culture and humanities (55), human services (51), community improvement and capacity building (44). By type of exemption the largest groups are social and recreational club (892), charitable, educational, religious and similar organization (681), business league, chamber of commerce and board of trade (41). The share of Carmel in the state's 44,752 organizations is 4%.

How large they report themselves to be

All 1,671 organizations in Carmel carry an income code from the latest return. Of those, 3% report income under $25,000, 18% report $25,000 to $499,999, 8% report $500,000 to $4,999,999 and 3% report $5 million or more; 69% have income code 0, which the IRS table lists as $0; that covers organizations that reported no income and probably also organizations that have no return data in the file, so the file alone cannot tell the two apart. The bands are the IRS's own income codes for the most recent return, and a return can be a few years old. The IRS information sheet says amounts are missing for organizations that file only the small e-Postcard or that are not required to file.

Gifts, legal form and group ties

The file marks contributions as deductible for 689 of the organizations (41%) and as not deductible for 925 (55%). By type of organization in the file: 874 associations, 773 corporations, 20 trusts. 734 are independent organizations and 918 are subordinate units in a group ruling, such as a local branch of a larger body.

The foundation code, which the IRS uses for 501(c)(3) organizations, marks 508 organizations that get a substantial part of their support from the public or government, 103 private foundations, 55 churches, 9 schools, 4 supporting organizations. These are the IRS's own classes and the file does not describe an organization's work.

When the exemption was recognised

The ruling date is the month and year of the IRS letter that recognised the exemption, which is not the year the organization was founded. 385 of the 1,671 organizations with a ruling date (23%) were recognised in 2016 or later. The decades with the most rulings are the 1940s (789), the 2020s (276), the 2010s (217).

By filing requirement code: 650 must file Form 990 or 990-EZ, 853 need only file the small Form 990-N (income under $50,000 a year, in the IRS's words), and 165 are marked as not required to file (churches, religious organizations, government bodies and others).
