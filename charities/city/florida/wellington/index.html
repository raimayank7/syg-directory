Tax-exempt organizations in Wellington, Florida

331 organizations

The IRS file has 331 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Wellington, Florida.

What kinds of organizations

By field of work, 52 have no code and the coded ones are mostly human services (40), philanthropy, voluntarism and grantmaking foundations (33), education (31), recreation and sports (28), religion-related (24), animal-related (20), arts, culture and humanities (17). By type of exemption the largest groups are charitable, educational, religious and similar organization (312), business league, chamber of commerce and board of trade (6), civic league and social welfare organization (5). The share of Wellington in the state's 115,205 organizations is 0%.

How large they report themselves to be

All 331 organizations in Wellington carry an income code from the latest return. Of those, 7% report income under $25,000, 21% report $25,000 to $499,999, 8% report $500,000 to $4,999,999 and 0% report $5 million or more; 63% have income code 0, which the IRS table lists as $0; that covers organizations that reported no income and probably also organizations that have no return data in the file, so the file alone cannot tell the two apart. The bands are the IRS's own income codes for the most recent return, and a return can be a few years old. The IRS information sheet says amounts are missing for organizations that file only the small e-Postcard or that are not required to file.

Gifts, legal form and group ties

The file marks contributions as deductible for 316 of the organizations (95%) and as not deductible for 13 (4%). By type of organization in the file: 294 corporations, 28 associations, 9 trusts. 299 are independent organizations and 32 are subordinate units in a group ruling, such as a local branch of a larger body.

The foundation code, which the IRS uses for 501(c)(3) organizations, marks 242 organizations that get a substantial part of their support from the public or government, 35 private foundations, 32 churches, 3 schools. These are the IRS's own classes and the file does not describe an organization's work.

When the exemption was recognised

The ruling date is the month and year of the IRS letter that recognised the exemption, which is not the year the organization was founded. 197 of the 331 organizations with a ruling date (60%) were recognised in 2016 or later. The decades with the most rulings are the 2020s (148), the 2010s (92), the 2000s (36).

By filing requirement code: 74 must file Form 990 or 990-EZ, 192 need only file the small Form 990-N (income under $50,000 a year, in the IRS's words), and 65 are marked as not required to file (churches, religious organizations, government bodies and others).
