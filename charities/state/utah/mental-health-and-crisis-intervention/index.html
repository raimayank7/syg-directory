Mental health and crisis intervention organizations in Utah

345 organizations

The IRS file has 345 mental health and crisis intervention organizations in Utah (NTEE major group F): 2.6% of the state's 13,228 tax-exempt organizations. For 296 of them the IRS has amounts: together they report $310.1 million of income.

How large they report themselves to be

All 345 mental health and crisis intervention organizations in Utah carry an income code from the latest return. Of those, 8% report income under $25,000, 17% report $25,000 to $499,999, 8% report $500,000 to $4,999,999 and 3% report $5 million or more; 63% have income code 0, which the IRS table lists as $0; that covers organizations that reported no income and probably also organizations that have no return data in the file, so the file alone cannot tell the two apart. The bands are the IRS's own income codes for the most recent return, and a return can be a few years old. The IRS information sheet says amounts are missing for organizations that file only the small e-Postcard or that are not required to file.

Gifts, legal form and group ties

The file marks contributions as deductible for 341 of the organizations (99%) and as not deductible for 3 (1%). By type of organization in the file: 335 corporations, 6 associations, 4 trusts. 344 are independent organizations and 1 are subordinate units in a group ruling, such as a local branch of a larger body.

The foundation code, which the IRS uses for 501(c)(3) organizations, marks 316 organizations that get a substantial part of their support from the public or government, 24 private foundations, 1 supporting organizations, 1 hospitals or medical research organizations. These are the IRS's own classes and the file does not describe an organization's work.

When the exemption was recognised

The ruling date is the month and year of the IRS letter that recognised the exemption, which is not the year the organization was founded. 274 of the 345 organizations with a ruling date (79%) were recognised in 2016 or later. The decades with the most rulings are the 2020s (235), the 2010s (67), the 2000s (23).

By filing requirement code: 93 must file Form 990 or 990-EZ, 227 need only file the small Form 990-N (income under $50,000 a year, in the IRS's words), and 25 are marked as not required to file (churches, religious organizations, government bodies and others).

Types of exemption and towns

By IRS subsection, these organizations are 342 charitable, educational, religious and similar organization, 2 business league, chamber of commerce and board of trade, 1 civic league and social welfare organization. The towns with the most of them are Salt Lake Cty (51), Provo (18), Ogden (15), Sandy (13), Park City (12). Counts are of organizations at the address on file with the IRS.
