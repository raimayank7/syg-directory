Health care organizations in Colorado

934 organizations

The IRS file has 934 health care organizations in Colorado (NTEE major group E): 2.6% of the state's 36,614 tax-exempt organizations. For 814 of them the IRS has amounts: together they report $28.89 billion of income.

How large they report themselves to be

All 934 health care organizations in Colorado carry an income code from the latest return. Of those, 5% report income under $25,000, 19% report $25,000 to $499,999, 13% report $500,000 to $4,999,999 and 12% report $5 million or more; 51% have income code 0, which the IRS table lists as $0; that covers organizations that reported no income and probably also organizations that have no return data in the file, so the file alone cannot tell the two apart. The bands are the IRS's own income codes for the most recent return, and a return can be a few years old. The IRS information sheet says amounts are missing for organizations that file only the small e-Postcard or that are not required to file.

Gifts, legal form and group ties

The file marks contributions as deductible for 910 of the organizations (97%) and as not deductible for 24 (3%). By type of organization in the file: 860 corporations, 68 associations, 4 trusts. 891 are independent organizations and 37 are subordinate units in a group ruling, such as a local branch of a larger body.

The foundation code, which the IRS uses for 501(c)(3) organizations, marks 760 organizations that get a substantial part of their support from the public or government, 63 hospitals or medical research organizations, 46 supporting organizations, 38 private foundations, 3 churches. These are the IRS's own classes and the file does not describe an organization's work.

When the exemption was recognised

The ruling date is the month and year of the IRS letter that recognised the exemption, which is not the year the organization was founded. 508 of the 934 organizations with a ruling date (54%) were recognised in 2016 or later. The decades with the most rulings are the 2020s (371), the 2010s (262), the 2000s (143).

By filing requirement code: 386 must file Form 990 or 990-EZ, 487 need only file the small Form 990-N (income under $50,000 a year, in the IRS's words), and 60 are marked as not required to file (churches, religious organizations, government bodies and others).

Types of exemption and towns

By IRS subsection, these organizations are 911 charitable, educational, religious and similar organization, 14 civic league and social welfare organization, 9 business league, chamber of commerce and board of trade. The towns with the most of them are Denver (154), Aurora (79), Colorado Spgs (68), Boulder (38), Centennial (25). Counts are of organizations at the address on file with the IRS.
