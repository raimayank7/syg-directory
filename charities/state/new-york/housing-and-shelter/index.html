Housing and shelter organizations in New York

2,391 organizations

The IRS file has 2,391 housing and shelter organizations in New York (NTEE major group L): 1.9% of the state's 125,167 tax-exempt organizations. For 2,170 of them the IRS has amounts: together they report $5.24 billion of income.

How large they report themselves to be

All 2,391 housing and shelter organizations in New York carry an income code from the latest return. Of those, 4% report income under $25,000, 23% report $25,000 to $499,999, 27% report $500,000 to $4,999,999 and 7% report $5 million or more; 38% have income code 0, which the IRS table lists as $0; that covers organizations that reported no income and probably also organizations that have no return data in the file, so the file alone cannot tell the two apart. The bands are the IRS's own income codes for the most recent return, and a return can be a few years old. The IRS information sheet says amounts are missing for organizations that file only the small e-Postcard or that are not required to file.

Gifts, legal form and group ties

The file marks contributions as deductible for 2,089 of the organizations (87%) and as not deductible for 298 (12%). By type of organization in the file: 2,309 corporations, 68 associations, 9 trusts. 2,304 are independent organizations and 75 are subordinate units in a group ruling, such as a local branch of a larger body.

The foundation code, which the IRS uses for 501(c)(3) organizations, marks 1,870 organizations that get a substantial part of their support from the public or government, 149 supporting organizations, 54 private foundations, 6 churches, 1 hospitals or medical research organizations. These are the IRS's own classes and the file does not describe an organization's work.

When the exemption was recognised

The ruling date is the month and year of the IRS letter that recognised the exemption, which is not the year the organization was founded. 900 of the 2,391 organizations with a ruling date (38%) were recognised in 2016 or later. The decades with the most rulings are the 2020s (651), the 2010s (619), the 1990s (397).

By filing requirement code: 1,371 must file Form 990 or 990-EZ, 937 need only file the small Form 990-N (income under $50,000 a year, in the IRS's words), and 79 are marked as not required to file (churches, religious organizations, government bodies and others).

Types of exemption and towns

By IRS subsection, these organizations are 2,086 charitable, educational, religious and similar organization, 280 civic league and social welfare organization, 11 title-holding corporation. The towns with the most of them are New York (651), Brooklyn (247), Bronx (202), Rochester (94), Buffalo (78). Counts are of organizations at the address on file with the IRS.
