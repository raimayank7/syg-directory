Tax-exempt organizations in Indiana

44,752 organizations

The IRS file has 44,752 tax-exempt organizations with an address in Indiana, 2.3% of the 1,975,209 counted on this site. 35,021 of them (78.3%) are 501(c)(3) organizations. For 33,530 of them the IRS has amounts from a return: together they report $120.23 billion of income and $317.15 billion of assets.

How large they report themselves to be

All 44,752 organizations in Indiana carry an income code from the latest return. Of those, 3% report income under $25,000, 19% report $25,000 to $499,999, 8% report $500,000 to $4,999,999 and 3% report $5 million or more; 67% have income code 0, which the IRS table lists as $0; that covers organizations that reported no income and probably also organizations that have no return data in the file, so the file alone cannot tell the two apart. The bands are the IRS's own income codes for the most recent return, and a return can be a few years old. The IRS information sheet says amounts are missing for organizations that file only the small e-Postcard or that are not required to file.

Gifts, legal form and group ties

The file marks contributions as deductible for 37,101 of the organizations (83%) and as not deductible for 6,887 (15%). By type of organization in the file: 32,797 corporations, 10,959 associations, 808 trusts, 23 co-operatives. 31,925 are independent organizations and 12,423 are subordinate units in a group ruling, such as a local branch of a larger body.

The foundation code, which the IRS uses for 501(c)(3) organizations, marks 24,938 organizations that get a substantial part of their support from the public or government, 6,991 churches, 1,832 private foundations, 624 supporting organizations, 456 schools. These are the IRS's own classes and the file does not describe an organization's work.

When the exemption was recognised

The ruling date is the month and year of the IRS letter that recognised the exemption, which is not the year the organization was founded. 15,789 of the 44,752 organizations with a ruling date (35%) were recognised in 2016 or later. The decades with the most rulings are the 2020s (11,635), the 2010s (9,673), the 2000s (4,613).

By filing requirement code: 12,025 must file Form 990 or 990-EZ, 23,394 need only file the small Form 990-N (income under $50,000 a year, in the IRS's words), and 9,263 are marked as not required to file (churches, religious organizations, government bodies and others).
