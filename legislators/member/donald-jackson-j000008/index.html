Donald Jackson

Representative, California · Republican · 1947 to 1961

Donald Jackson was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 7 terms, the first starting on 3 January 1947 and the last ending on 3 January 1961. All the terms were in the House of Representatives. The member represented California. The party is filed as Republican. In all, the terms add up to 5,108 days (about 14.0 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
