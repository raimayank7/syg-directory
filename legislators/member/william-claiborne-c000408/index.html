William Claiborne

Senator, Louisiana · Democratic Republican · 1797 to 1817

William Claiborne was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 3 terms, the first starting on 15 May 1797 and the last ending on 31 December 1817. The member served in both chambers: 2 terms in the House of Representatives and 1 term in the Senate. The member represented Tennessee and Louisiana. The party is filed as Republican and then Democratic Republican. In all, the terms add up to 1,143 days (about 3.1 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
