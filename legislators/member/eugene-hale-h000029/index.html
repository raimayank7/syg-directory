Eugene Hale

Senator, Maine · Republican · 1869 to 1911

Eugene Hale was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 10 terms, the first starting on 4 March 1869 and the last ending on 3 March 1911. The member served in both chambers: 5 terms in the House of Representatives and 5 terms in the Senate. The member represented Maine. The party is filed as Republican. In all, the terms add up to 12,566 days (about 34.4 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
