Carolyn McCarthy

Representative, New York · Democrat · 1997 to 2015

Carolyn McCarthy was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 9 terms, the first starting on 7 January 1997 and the last ending on 3 January 2015. All the terms were in the House of Representatives. The member represented New York. The party is filed as Democrat. In all, the terms add up to 6,556 days (about 17.9 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
