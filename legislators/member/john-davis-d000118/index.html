John Davis

Representative, Indiana · Anti-Lecompton Democrat · 1851 to 1861

John Davis was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 4 terms, the first starting on 1 December 1851 and the last ending on 3 March 1861. All the terms were in the House of Representatives. The member represented Indiana. The party is filed as Democrat and then Anti-Lecompton Democrat.
