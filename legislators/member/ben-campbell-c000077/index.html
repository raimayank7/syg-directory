Ben Campbell

Senator, Colorado · Republican · 1987 to 2005

Ben Campbell was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 5 terms, the first starting on 6 January 1987 and the last ending on 3 January 2005. The member served in both chambers: 3 terms in the House of Representatives and 2 terms in the Senate. The member represented Colorado. The party is filed as Democrat and then Republican. In all, the terms add up to 6,567 days (about 18.0 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
