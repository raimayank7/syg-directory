Hiland Hall

Representative, Vermont · Whig · 1831 to 1843

Hiland Hall was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 6 terms, the first starting on 5 December 1831 and the last ending on 3 March 1843. All the terms were in the House of Representatives. The member represented Vermont. The party is filed as Anti Jacksonian and then Whig. In all, the terms add up to 3,005 days (about 8.2 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
