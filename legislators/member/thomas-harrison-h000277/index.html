Thomas Harrison

Representative, Virginia · Democrat · 1915 to 1929

All the terms were in the House of Representatives. The member represented Virginia. The party is filed as Democrat. In all, the terms add up to 3,860 days (about 10.6 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
