John Thomson

Representative, Ohio · Jackson · 1825 to 1837

John Thomson was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 5 terms, the first starting on 5 December 1825 and the last ending on 3 March 1837. All the terms were in the House of Representatives. The member represented Ohio. The party is filed as Jackson. In all, the terms add up to 2,266 days (about 6.2 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
