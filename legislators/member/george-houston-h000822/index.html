George Houston

Senator, Alabama · Democrat · 1841 to 1879

George Houston was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 10 terms, the first starting on 31 May 1841 and the last ending on 31 December 1879. The member served in both chambers: 9 terms in the House of Representatives and 1 term in the Senate. The member represented Alabama. The party is filed as Democrat. In all, the terms add up to 4,566 days (about 12.5 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
