William McKinley

Senator, Illinois · Republican · 1905 to 1927

William McKinley was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 8 terms, the first starting on 4 December 1905 and the last ending on 4 March 1927. The member served in both chambers: 7 terms in the House of Representatives and 1 term in the Senate. The member represented Illinois. The party is filed as Republican. In all, the terms add up to 6,288 days (about 17.2 years) from the start dates to their end dates.

Born 5 September 1856, gender filed as male.
