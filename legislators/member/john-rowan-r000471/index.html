John Rowan

Senator, Kentucky · Jackson · 1807 to 1831

John Rowan was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 2 terms, the first starting on 26 October 1807 and the last ending on 3 March 1831. The member served in both chambers: 1 term in the House of Representatives and 1 term in the Senate. The member represented Kentucky. The party is filed as Republican and then Jackson. In all, the terms add up to 2,408 days (about 6.6 years) from the start dates to their end dates.

Born 12 July 1773, gender filed as male.
