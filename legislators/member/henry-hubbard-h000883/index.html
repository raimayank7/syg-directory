Henry Hubbard

Senator, New Hampshire · Democrat · 1829 to 1841

Henry Hubbard was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 4 terms, the first starting on 7 December 1829 and the last ending on 3 March 1841. The member served in both chambers: 3 terms in the House of Representatives and 1 term in the Senate. The member represented New Hampshire. The party is filed as Jackson and then Democrat. In all, the terms add up to 3,274 days (about 9.0 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
