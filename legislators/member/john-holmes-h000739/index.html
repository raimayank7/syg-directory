John Holmes

Senator, Maine · Adams · 1817 to 1833

John Holmes was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 5 terms, the first starting on 1 December 1817 and the last ending on 3 March 1833. The member served in both chambers: 2 terms in the House of Representatives and 3 terms in the Senate. The member represented Massachusetts and Maine. The party is filed as Democratic Republican and then Adams. In all, the terms add up to 4,516 days (about 12.4 years) from the start dates to their end dates.

Born 14 March 1773, gender filed as male.
