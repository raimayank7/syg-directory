Hugo Black

Senator, Alabama · Democrat · 1927 to 1937

Hugo Black was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 2 terms, the first starting on 5 December 1927 and the last ending on 19 August 1937. All the terms were in the Senate. The member represented Alabama. The party is filed as Democrat. In all, the terms add up to 3,539 days (about 9.7 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
