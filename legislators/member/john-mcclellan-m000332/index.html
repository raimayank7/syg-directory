John McClellan

Senator, Arkansas · Democrat · 1935 to 1977

John McClellan was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 8 terms, the first starting on 3 January 1935 and the last ending on 28 November 1977. The member served in both chambers: 2 terms in the House of Representatives and 6 terms in the Senate. The member represented Arkansas. The party is filed as Democrat. In all, the terms add up to 14,195 days (about 38.9 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
