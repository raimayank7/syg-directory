John Moore

Representative, Louisiana · Whig · 1839 to 1853

John Moore was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 3 terms, the first starting on 2 December 1839 and the last ending on 3 March 1853. All the terms were in the House of Representatives. The member represented Louisiana. The party is filed as Whig. In all, the terms add up to 1,556 days (about 4.3 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
