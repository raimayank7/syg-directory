Steve Daines

Senator, Montana · Republican · 2013 to present

Steve Daines is a member of the US Congress. The data set records 3 terms, the first starting on 3 January 2013 and the latest running to 3 January 2027. The member served in both chambers: 1 term in the House of Representatives and 2 terms in the Senate. The member represented Montana. The party is filed as Republican. In all, the terms add up to 5,060 days (about 13.9 years) from the start dates to today.
