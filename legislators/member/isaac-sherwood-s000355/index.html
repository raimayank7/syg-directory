Isaac Sherwood

Representative, Ohio · Democrat · 1873 to 1925

Isaac Sherwood was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 9 terms, the first starting on 1 December 1873 and the last ending on 3 March 1925. All the terms were in the House of Representatives. The member represented Ohio. The party is filed as Republican and then Democrat. In all, the terms add up to 5,289 days (about 14.5 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
