Everette Howard

Representative, Oklahoma · Democrat · 1919 to 1929

Everette Howard was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 3 terms, the first starting on 19 May 1919 and the last ending on 3 March 1929. All the terms were in the House of Representatives. The member represented Oklahoma. The party is filed as Democrat. In all, the terms add up to 1,564 days (about 4.3 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
