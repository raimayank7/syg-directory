Shelby Cullom

Senator, Illinois · Republican · 1865 to 1913

Shelby Cullom was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 8 terms, the first starting on 4 December 1865 and the last ending on 3 March 1913. The member served in both chambers: 3 terms in the House of Representatives and 5 terms in the Senate. The member represented Illinois. The party is filed as Republican. In all, the terms add up to 11,499 days (about 31.5 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
