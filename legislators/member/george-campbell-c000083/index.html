George Campbell

Senator, Tennessee · Republican · 1803 to 1819

George Campbell was a member of the US Congress. The data set records 5 terms, the first starting on 17 October 1803 and the last ending on 3 March 1819. The member served in both chambers: 3 terms in the House of Representatives and 2 terms in the Senate. The member represented Tennessee. The party is filed as Republican. In all, the terms add up to 3,853 days (about 10.5 years) from the start dates to their end dates.
